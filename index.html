CASE STUDY: BEHAVIOUR & RETROFITTING OF A SLENDER RC COLUMN DUE TO BEAM REMOVAL IN A G+2 BUILDING

PROJECT SITE: DHARMAPURI

Structural Modeling & Analysis (ETABS)

STATUS: OVERSTRESSED
ETABS 3D FEA Analysis
Original Section: 230 x 300 mm KL/d = 28.53 (Limit: 12)
  • Altered Load Transfer: Analysed critical behavior of slender C7 column (230×300 mm) subjected to doubled unsupported length (6.10m total) following beam removal in G+2 frame.
  • Extreme Slenderness: Major axis slenderness ratio kL/d = 28.53 and minor axis 13.56 both exceeded the code maximum limit of 12.0 (IS 456 Annex E).
  • Biaxial Failure: High biaxial interaction demand (Pu = 536.8 kN, Mu3 = 52.28 kNm) caused severe overstress.
Biaxial Interaction Demand
CR: 1.179 (Exceeds 1.0 Limit)
NOT ADEQUATE

Retrofitting & Modification (RC Jacketing)

STATUS: DESIGN PASS
Cross-Section 380 x 380 mm
Jacketing Concrete: M25 Grade Longitudinal Steel: 3,045 mm²
  • Encasement Engineering: Designed an integrated 380×380 mm reinforced concrete jacket utilizing 16mm longitudinal bars, 8mm lateral ties, and chemical shear dowels.
  • Slenderness Rectification: Increased moment of inertia drastically reduced slenderness to 9.63 < 12, transforming the member into a stocky, non-buckling column.
  • Sustainable Rehabilitation: Prevented building demolition and structural failure while satisfying IS 456 biaxial interaction parameters.

From ETABS Model to Retrofit: Column C7 Step by Step

Scroll through the stacked deck to follow column C7 from original 3D geometry to load application, live sway deformation, code verification failure, and jacketed restoration.

01 MODEL ETABS 3D FRAME

The Existing Structure

A G+2 residential structure in Dharmapuri, modelled in ETABS with 3050 mm typical storey height. Critical column C7 has initial dimensions of 230 × 300 mm, framing beams are 230 × 450 mm spanning up to 6350 mm, with a cantilever balcony slab.

C7 COLUMN:230 × 300 mm
FRAMING BEAMS:230 × 450 mm
MAX BEAM SPAN:6350 mm
STOREY HEIGHT:3.05 m

Removing the 1st-floor beam eliminates lateral restraint at mid-height, causing double-height buckling behavior in C7.

02 LOADS 11.96 / 5.52 KN/M

Applied Member Loads

Gravity line loads applied uniformly to peripheral and internal framing beams according to IS 875:

FLOOR LINE LOAD:11.96 kN/m
ROOF LINE LOAD:5.52 kN/m
Analysis under 1.5(DL + LL) combination produces axial load Pu = 536.8 kN, major moment Mu3 = 52.28 kNm, and minor moment Mu2 = 18.93 kNm.
03 DEFORM LIVE MODEL

Deformed Shape & Sway

Drag the slider to simulate 3D space frame sway and beam curvature under gravity and lateral load transfer.

AXIAL (Pu) 536.8 kN
MAJOR (Mu3) 52.28 kNm
MINOR (Mu2) 18.93 kNm

Without first-floor beam restraint, column C7 buckles across a doubled 5.65 m unsupported length.

04 SLENDERNESS LIMIT 12
Major axis (kL/D) 28.53
Minor axis (kL/b) 13.57
Dashed line: slenderness limit of 12.
Major: K 1.515 × Lu 5650 mm / D 300 mm = 28.53.
Minor: K 1.200 × Lu 2600 mm / b 230 mm = 13.57.

The Column Becomes Slender

Without the beam, the unsupported length of C7 about the major axis grows to 5650 mm, a length factor of 1.852. Wood’s chart for a sway frame gives K = 1.515.

28.53

Both ratios exceed 12, so C7 is a slender column.

05 CAPACITY CR 1.179
Capacity Ratio of C7 1.179
Mu3 / M3,cap + Mu2 / M2,cap
Major: 52.28 / 63.32 = 0.826
Minor: 18.93 / 53.49 = 0.354
Demand exceeds capacity: NOT ADEQUATE (OVERSTRESSED)

Capacity Check to IS 456

Slenderness adds secondary moment to C7: 47.3 kNm about the major axis and 8.2 kNm about the minor axis, after a reduction factor k of 0.721. The biaxial interaction check combines both axes.

1.179

Capacity ratio, above 1.0, so the 230 × 300 mm section is not adequate.

06 RETROFIT CR 0.986 PASS
Before: 1.179
After: 0.986

Retrofit with RC Jacketing

Jacketing enlarges C7 to 380 × 380 mm, which makes it a short column in both directions (slenderness 9.63). The jacket keeps the existing core and adds new Ø16 mm longitudinal bars, Ø8 mm links around it, and Ø8 mm dowel bars tying new concrete to old.

0.986

Capacity ratio after jacketing, at the design limit (IS 456 compliant).

Passes the checked load combination. Shoring, dowel anchorage, interface preparation, and foundation capacity still verified on site.
