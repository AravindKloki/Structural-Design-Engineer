CASE STUDY: BEHAVIOUR & RETROFITTING OF A SLENDER RC COLUMN DUE TO BEAM REMOVAL IN A G+2 BUILDING

PROJECT SITE: DHARMAPURI

Structural Modeling & Analysis (ETABS)

STATUS: OVERSTRESSED
ETABS 3D FEA Analysis
Original Section: 230 x 300 mm KL/d = 28.53 (Limit: 12)
  • Altered Load Transfer: Analysed critical behavior of slender C7 column (230×300 mm) subjected to doubled unsupported length (6.10m total) following beam removal in G+2 frame.
  • Extreme Slenderness: Major axis slenderness ratio kL/d = 28.53 and minor axis 13.56 both exceeded the code maximum limit of 12.0 (IS 456 Annex E).
  • Biaxial Failure: High biaxial interaction demand (Pu = 536.8 kN, Mu3 = 52.28 kNm) caused severe overstress.
Biaxial Interaction Demand
CR: 1.179 (Exceeds 1.0 Limit)
NOT ADEQUATE

Retrofitting & Modification (RC Jacketing)

STATUS: DESIGN PASS
Cross-Section 380 x 380 mm
Jacketing Concrete: M25 Grade Longitudinal Steel: 3,045 mm²
  • Encasement Engineering: Designed an integrated 380×380 mm reinforced concrete jacket utilizing 16mm longitudinal bars, 8mm lateral ties, and chemical shear dowels.
  • Slenderness Rectification: Increased moment of inertia drastically reduced slenderness to 9.63 < 12, transforming the member into a stocky, non-buckling column.
  • Sustainable Rehabilitation: Prevented building demolition and structural failure while satisfying IS 456 biaxial interaction parameters.

CR: 0.986 (Pass, at design limit)

The existing frame

A G+2 residential building in Dharmapuri, modelled in ETABS with a 3050 mm storey height. Column C7 is 230 x 300 mm, the beams are 230 x 450 mm and span up to 6350 mm, and one slab cantilevers without back-span support.

Removing the first-floor beam changes how load reaches C7, so this model is the starting point for every check.

Loads applied

Line loads of 11.96 kN/m act on the floors and 5.52 kN/m on the roof.

The analysis gives C7 an axial load of 536.8 kN, a major-axis moment Mu3 of 52.28 kNm and a minor-axis moment Mu2 of 18.93 kNm.

Deformed shape

Scroll through this stage, or drag the slider, to deform the frame live. The ETABS result is one click away.

Deflection is exaggerated for clarity. The frame is a reconstruction of the ETABS model grid.

The column becomes slender

Without the beam, the unsupported length of C7 about the major axis grows to 5650 mm, a length factor of 1.852. Wood’s chart for a sway frame gives K = 1.515.

28.53 major, limit 12

13.57 minor, limit 12

Capacity check to IS 456

Slenderness adds moment to C7: 47.3 kNm about the major axis and 8.2 kNm about the minor axis, after a reduction factor k of 0.721. The biaxial interaction check combines both axes.

CR 1.179 above 1.0: not adequate

Retrofit with RC jacketing

The jacket keeps the existing core and adds new Ø16 mm longitudinal bars, Ø8 mm links around it and Ø8 mm dowel bars tying new concrete to old. At 380 x 380 mm, C7 becomes a short column (slenderness 9.63).

CR 0.986 at the design limit

This passes only the checked load combination. A three-sided jacket may not act like the full 380 x 380 mm section, so shoring, dowel anchorage, interface preparation and foundation capacity still need verifying.
