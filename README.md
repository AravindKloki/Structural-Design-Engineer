## 🏗️ Featured Projects & Case Studies

### 1. Behavior & Retrofitting of a Slender RC Column (G+2 Building)
- **Problem:** Removal of a first-floor beam increased unsupported column length, leading to severe slenderness ($kL/d > 12$) and demand exceeding capacity ($\text{CR} = 1.179$).
- **Solution:** Designed an RC jacket enlargement from $230 \times 300\text{ mm}$ to $380 \times 380\text{ mm}$ with dowel anchors and confinement links.
- **Result:** Reduced slenderness to short-column behavior and lowered capacity ratio to $0.986$ (within design limits).

### 2. Structural Design of a Community Hall
- Prepared architectural/structural drawings in AutoCAD and built 3D frame models in STAAD.Pro to evaluate member forces and gravity/lateral load actions.

### 3. Lead Removal via Electrocoagulation
- Environmental engineering research evaluating the efficiency of electrochemical precipitation in treating heavy metals from wastewater effluents.
