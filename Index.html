CASE STUDY: BEHAVIOUR & RETROFITTING OF A SLENDER RC COLUMN DUE TO BEAM REMOVAL IN A G+2 BUILDING

PROJECT SITE: DHARMAPURI

Structural Modeling & Analysis (ETABS)

STATUS: OVERSTRESSED
ETABS 3D FEA Analysis
Original Section: 230 x 300 mm KL/d = 28.53 (Limit: 12)
  • Altered Load Transfer: Analysed critical behavior of slender C7 column (230×300 mm) subjected to doubled unsupported length (6.10m total) following beam removal in G+2 frame.
  • Extreme Slenderness: Major axis slenderness ratio kL/d = 28.53 and minor axis 13.56 both exceeded the code maximum limit of 12.0 (IS 456 Annex E).
  • Biaxial Failure: High biaxial interaction demand (Pu = 536.8 kN, Mu3 = 52.28 kNm) caused severe overstress.
Biaxial Interaction Demand
CR: 1.179 (Exceeds 1.0 Limit)
NOT ADEQUATE

Retrofitting & Modification (RC Jacketing)

STATUS: DESIGN PASS
Cross-Section 380 x 380 mm
Jacketing Concrete: M25 Grade Longitudinal Steel: 3,045 mm²
  • Encasement Engineering: Designed an integrated 380×380 mm reinforced concrete jacket utilizing 16mm longitudinal bars, 8mm lateral ties, and chemical shear dowels.
  • Slenderness Rectification: Increased moment of inertia drastically reduced slenderness to 9.63 < 12, transforming the member into a stocky, non-buckling column.
  • Sustainable Rehabilitation: Prevented building demolition and structural failure while satisfying IS 456 biaxial interaction parameters.
